# Module 04: AI Proposal & Document Generator

This module transforms minimal inputs (short forms, voice notes, bullet points) into massive, professionally formatted PDF proposals, Quotations, and Statements of Work (SOW).

## 📈 Business Impact & ROI
*   **Eliminates Administrative Bottlenecks:** Saves account managers and founders 2-4 hours per client by automating the tedious drafting phase of proposals.
*   **Standardized Quality:** Ensures every outbound proposal maintains a high standard of professional copywriting and formatting, regardless of which junior employee triggered the workflow.
*   **Faster Sales Cycles:** Getting a custom, detailed proposal into a client's hands within 5 minutes of a discovery call drastically increases closing momentum.

## 🏗️ System Architecture & Importance
*   **Dynamic Data Binding:** Ingests unstructured inputs, uses the LLM to structure them, and binds that data to dynamic HTML templates before converting to a secure PDF payload.
*   **Database Integration:** Designed to pull real-time pricing data from connected databases (Google Sheets/Supabase) to ensure calculations (e.g., Construction margins, SaaS tiering) are 100% mathematically accurate.
*   **Document Management Automation:** Automatically uploads generated assets to secure storage (Google Drive/AWS S3) and returns the public sharing link to the team via Slack/Airtable, creating a seamless audit trail.
